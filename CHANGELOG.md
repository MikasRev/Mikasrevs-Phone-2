# Changelog

## 0.9.4 beta

- Refreshed Marketplace branding, colors and layouts.
- Replaced Direct Sell with item trade requests, server custody, confirmation before approval and automatic delivery/returns to inventory.
- Added private trade chat outside the phone, improved text readability and prevented trading the phone currently in use.
- Added real-world/Minecraft clock selection and updated phone typography.
- Added and polished the admin panel: social-app bans/unbans and assigned moderators; fixed overlapping feedback text.
- Added Simplified and Traditional Chinese and expanded checks across all eleven translations.
- Integrated Kuro's Economy alongside Lightman's Currency. Phone transfers use existing balances without fees.
- Improved bank recovery after interrupted saves, duplicate-request handling, decimal amounts and world-specific pending transactions.
- Stabilized call animations, phone direction and hand placement in first- and third-person views.
- Improved YouTube mouse/input cleanup and other phone lifecycle, trade and persistence behavior.
- Retained the experimental iPhone Duo source while hiding its item from Creative inventory for future development.
